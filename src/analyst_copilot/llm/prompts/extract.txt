Find the evidence needed to answer the question, using ONLY the supplied pages.

Return evidence slots. Do NOT write a prose answer, and do NOT compute anything.
Each slot: name, value, unit, scale, period, doc_id, page_seq, page_printed, quote.

RULES, in priority order:
 1. `quote` MUST be copied VERBATIM from the supplied page text. It is checked
    character-by-character against the source; a paraphrase fails and the answer
    is discarded.
    The quote MUST CONTAIN THE FIGURE YOU PUT IN `value`, together with its
    line-item label, on the same line. A quote carrying only the label is
    useless: a reviewer who sees it cannot confirm the number, and the answer
    will be rejected. Copy the whole line, label and figures together, e.g.
      "Purchases of property, plant and equipment (PP&E) (1,577) (1,373) (1,420)"
    For a non-numeric answer, quote the full sentence that states the fact.
 2. Each page is introduced by a line of the form
      <<< doc_id=<ID> page_seq=<N> >>>
    Copy `doc_id` and `page_seq` EXACTLY from the marker above the page you
    quoted. Never construct, abbreviate or infer them.
 3. Never supply a figure from your own knowledge, and never adjust a figure to
    make it consistent. Report what the page says.
 4. Also return: answer_type (scalar|ratio|comparison|ranking|qualitative),
    metric_name if a named financial metric is requested, and any definition the
    QUESTION ITSELF supplies (this overrides our formula book).
 5. If a required value is absent from the supplied pages, list it in
    `missing_slots` and stop. Do not infer it, do not estimate it, and do not
    substitute a similar line item from a different period or statement.
