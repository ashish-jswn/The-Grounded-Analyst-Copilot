Extract retrieval metadata from an analyst question about SEC filings.

Return ONLY these fields:
  companies      : list of company names exactly as written in the question
  form_types     : any of ["10-K","10-Q","8-K"] the question implies; [] if unclear
  periods        : list of {fiscal_year:int, fiscal_quarter:int|null}
  intent         : "historical" | "forecast"
                   -- "forecast" when the question asks what the company expects,
                      forecasts, plans, projects, or guides toward. For forecast
                      intent the requested period may be AFTER the filing period.
  company_named  : true only if a company is explicitly named or aliased
  metric_name    : the financial metric asked for, as a SHORT NOUN PHRASE of at
                   most 4 words ("operating margin", "free cash flow"), or null
                   if no named metric is requested. Never explain your choice
                   in this field.

Do not guess a company that is not named. Do not infer a year that is not stated.
