a financial table. The units ("in millions", "in thousands") and the

almost never inside the quoted line itself. Resolve them from the page. "The
quote does not state its units" or "does not say which year this column is" is
NOT a defect when the page answers it — it is the normal shape of a filing.

   "in thousands" IS $15.0 million)
